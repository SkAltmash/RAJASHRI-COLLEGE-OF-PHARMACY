About Our College

Established in 2023, Rajashri College of Pharmacy (RCOP Mehkar) stands as a premier institution dedicated to professional pharmaceutical education in the Vidarbha and Marathwada regions. Since our inception, we have committed ourselves to fostering academic excellence, research capabilities, and ethical professional practices among aspiring pharmacists.

Our institution is recognized by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), New Delhi, and affiliated with the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai. We blend theoretical knowledge with hands-on laboratory experiences to shape students into industry-ready healthcare providers.

Why Choose RCOP Mehkar?

  • Academic Legacy: Shaping pharmacy education since 2023.
  • State-of-the-Art Labs & Infrastructure: Modern equipment for practical and research work.
  • Expert & Dedicated Faculty: Mentorship from experienced pharmaceutical professionals.
  • Holistic Student Development: Focus on industrial visits, soft skills, and placement assistance.
  • Commitment to Community Healthcare: Regular healthcare rallies and pharmacy awareness campaigns.

Our Journey

Our Journey of Excellence

A timeline of growth, quality milestones, and student achievements since our inception in 2023.

2023-24

The Beginning

Rajashri College of Pharmacy was established in Mehkar with the launch of the D.Pharm program (60 seats intake), addressing a critical need for pharmaceutical education in the Buldhana district.

2025-26

B.Pharm Expansion

Responding to positive community feedback and academic demand, the college introduced the 4-year Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) program, affiliated to the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), Mumbai.

2025

Advanced Lab Infrastructure

Invested in sophisticated instrument rooms, a modernized library, and high-tech computer laboratories, obtaining PCI approvals and MSBTE affiliations for enhanced seats.

2026

Academic MoUs & Collaborations

Signed MoUs with top pharmaceutical organizations and local healthcare facilities to boost training, internship opportunities, and placement drives.

2027+

Looking Ahead

Expanding our horizons, modernizing classroom technologies with interactive smart-boards, and launching community healthcare outreach initiatives.

Message from the Head of Department (HOD)

Head of Department
Academic & Practical Training Desk

Dear Students and Future Healthcare Leaders,

Welcome to the academic corridors of Rajashri College of Pharmacy, Mehkar.

While visionary leadership guides our institution's direction, the heart of our day-to-day work beats inside our lecture halls, analytical benches, and formulation laboratories. As the Head of Department, my commitment is to ensure that your hours spent here translate directly into working competence, precision, and confidence.

Pharmacy is an exact science where a fraction of a milligram can define patient outcomes. For this reason, our academic department treats every experiment, titration, formulation run, and dosage calculation as a direct rehearsal for your professional career. We focus relentlessly on:

  • Practical Precision: Moving beyond rote memorisation to instil true bench-level competence and strict Good Laboratory Practices (GLP).
  • Conceptual Clarity: Breaking down complex mechanisms of action, drug synthesis pathways, and pharmaceutical calculations into intuitive, applicable knowledge.
  • Continuous Mentorship: Providing accessible faculty support, periodic assessments, and academic counselling to ensure no student is left behind.

A pharmacy diploma or degree is not just an academic credential—it is a license of public trust. The habits of discipline, punctuality, and analytical honesty you build in our laboratories will determine how effectively you serve patients, doctors, and the pharmaceutical industry tomorrow.

Make every practical session count, question deeply, and respect the science you practice.

Our faculty team stands ready to mentor, challenge, and guide you through every stage of your technical training.

Best wishes for a rigorous and rewarding academic journey.

Dr. Harshal S. Anwane Dhote M. Pharm (Pharmaceutical Chemistry), PhD (Pharmaceutical Chemistry) Head of Department Rajashri College of Pharmacy Mehkar, Dist. Buldhana (M.S.)
