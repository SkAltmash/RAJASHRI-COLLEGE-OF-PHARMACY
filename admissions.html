How to Qualify

RCOP Mehkar offers admissions through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) of the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Maharashtra.

Program Intake Course Code Basic Eligibility
D.Pharm (Diploma) 60 Seats 127882310 10+2 Passed (Physics, Chemistry & Biology/Mathematics)
B.Pharm (Degree) 60 Seats 127882350 10+2 Passed (PCB/PCM) with min 45% aggregate (40% for reserve) + CET score
B.Pharm (Lateral Entry) Direct 2nd Yr - Passed D.Pharm program from a PCI approved institute with min 45%

Documents Checklist

Please ensure you have both original copies and 3 sets of attested xerox copies of the following documents during reporting:

  • SSC (10th) Marksheet
  • HSC (12th) Marksheet
  • MHT-CET / NEET Score Card
  • School / College Leaving Certificate
  • Indian Domicile Certificate
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Caste Validity Certificate
  • Non-Creamy Layer Certificate
  • Income Certificate (for EBC/TFWS)
  • Passport size photographs (5 copies)
  • Aadhar Card
  • Bank Passbook

CAP Rounds

Admission Steps (CAP)

The standard registration procedure for securing a seat through government rounds.

1️⃣

Online Registration

Register on the CET Portal, create login credentials, and submit the online application form.

2️⃣

FC Verification

Visit the nearest Facilitation Centre (FC) for physical document verification and confirmation.

3️⃣

Option Submission

Login to fill out CAP option forms, listing Rajashri College of Pharmacy, Mehkar as your top choice.

4️⃣

Seat Allotment

Confirm your allotted seat, pay the seat acceptance fee, and report to our college with documents.
