2-Year Diploma Program

Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm)

The D.Pharm program is a foundational course designed to prepare students for pharmacy practice in retail, hospital, and distribution sectors. The program is heavily focused on practical pharmaceutical techniques, basic medical sciences, and dispensing regulation.

Duration2 Years (Annual Pattern)
Intake Capacity60 Seats
Eligibility10+2 (Science Stream) with Physics, Chemistry and Biology / Mathematics.
AffiliationAffiliation MSBTE, Approved by DTE & PCI.

Syllabus Highlights

  • Pharmaceutics I & II
  • Pharmaceutical Chemistry
  • Pharmacognosy & Herbals
  • Biochemistry & Clinical Path.
  • Human Anatomy & Physiology
  • Health Education & Comm. Pharmacy
  • Pharmacology & Toxicology
  • Drug Store & Business Mgmt.

4-Year Degree Program

Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm)

B.Pharm is a highly comprehensive undergraduate program that offers deep insights into drug synthesis, formulation development, pharmacology, analytical chemistry, and regulatory practices. This degree is the gold standard for pursuing careers in pharmaceutical research, production, drug control, or academic settings.

Duration4 Years (8 Semesters)
Intake Capacity60 Seats
Eligibility10+2 (Science Stream) with PCB/PCM. Must qualify state-level MHT-CET or national NEET exam. Lateral Entry directly to 2nd year available for D.Pharm holders.
AffiliationAffiliation Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University, Approved by PCI & DTE Maharashtra.

Core Study Modules

  • Human Anatomy & Physiology
  • Pharmaceutical Chemistry
  • Physical Pharmaceutics
  • Pharmacology (Mechanism of Action)
  • Medicinal Chemistry
  • Industrial & Biopharmaceutics
  • Quality Control & Instrumentation
  • Novel Drug Delivery Systems (NDDS)
