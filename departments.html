Department of Pharmaceutics

Pharmaceutics Lab & Facilities

At Rajashri College of Pharmacy, the Department of Pharmaceutics specializes in training students in the art and science of formulation and dosage form design. Our state-of-the-art pharmaceutics laboratory and industrial-scale machine room provide students with hands-on experience in manufacturing tablets, capsules, ointments, and liquid dosage forms, fully aligning with current GMP standards and MSBTE curricula.

Key Equipment & Machine Room Facilities

  • Single punch tablet machine (Tablet Punching)
  • Dissolution test apparatus (USP/IP standard)
  • Disintegration tester & Friability test apparatus
  • Ampoule filling & sealing assembly
  • Colloid mill & Ball mill (Size reduction)
  • Brookfield Viscometer

Department of Pharmacology

Pharmacology Lab & Apparatus

The Department of Pharmacology at RCOP Mehkar focuses on providing students with deep insights into drug action, pharmacokinetics, and human physiology. Students perform practical training in our fully equipped APHE (Human Anatomy & Physiology) laboratory, utilizing advanced digital kymographs, organ baths, and simulated experimental software that ensures high-quality scientific screening while respecting animal-free testing guidelines.

Key Laboratories & Apparatus

  • APHE Lab (Human Anatomy, Physiology & Health Education)
  • Digital Kymographs & student organ baths
  • Rotarod apparatus (Muscle coordination testing)
  • Actophotometer (Locomotor activity screening)
  • Simulated Pharmacology experimental software (No animal cruelty)
  • Sphygmomanometers & Spirometers

Pharmaceutical Chemistry

Chemistry Lab & Instruments

Our Pharmaceutical Chemistry department prepares students for advanced active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) analysis, drug synthesis, and quality control protocols. The Sophisticated Instrument Room is equipped with modern analytical tools, including HPLC and UV-Visible spectrophotometers, giving B.Pharm students direct exposure to industry-grade research, purification, and molecular validation methods.

Sophisticated Analytical Instrument Room

  • High Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC)
  • Double-beam UV-Visible Spectrophotometer
  • Abbe's Refractometer & Digital Polarimeter
  • Karl-Fischer moisture titrator
  • Muffle Furnace & Digital melting point apparatus
  • Fume Hoods for safe synthetic procedures

Department of Pharmacognosy

Pharmacognosy Lab & Garden

The Department of Pharmacognosy at RCOP Mehkar specializes in the study of natural drug sources and traditional herbal medicines. Our students engage in phytoconstituent extraction, plant tissue identification, and microscopic validation in our specialized labs. The department maintains a rich campus Medicinal Plant Garden with over 50 active therapeutic species and a dedicated Drug Museum.

Pharmacognosy Lab & Museum Features

  • Soxhlet extraction assemblies (Phytoconstituent extraction)
  • Compound microscopes & Camera lucida
  • Drug Museum with 200+ crude drug specimens
  • Medicinal Plant Garden (50+ active species)
  • Crude drug classification charts & slide library
  • Maceration assemblies & Chromatography jars
