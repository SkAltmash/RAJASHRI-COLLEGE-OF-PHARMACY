About RCOP Mehkar

About Us

Rajashri College of Pharmacy, Mehkar is a premier institution dedicated to pharmaceutical education in the Vidarbha and Marathwada region. Recognized by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) and affiliated with the Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE), we offer world-class education blended with strong ethical values.

Our mission is to produce competent, compassionate, and innovative pharmacy professionals who contribute meaningfully to society and the healthcare sector.

  • MSBTE Affiliated & PCI Recognized
  • Affiliated to the Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University
  • State-of-the-art pharmaceutical laboratories
  • Experienced & dedicated faculty members
  • Strong industry-academia partnerships
  • Scholarship opportunities for meritorious students
