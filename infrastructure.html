Learning Environment

World-Class Campus Facilities

Providing the resources, instruments, and spaces required to nurture top-tier healthcare professionals.

🧪

Modern Laboratories

12+ fully equipped laboratories with standard gas connections, safety exhaust systems, emergency eye-washes, and high-quality reagents.

📚

Central Library

10,000+ volumes, reference handbooks, national and international print journals, e-journal subscriptions, and quiet study cabins.

🏛️

Seminar Hall

Spacious air-conditioned seminar hall with a seating capacity of 300+ guests, equipped with digital AV projectors and sound systems.

🏠

Smart Classroom

Modern ICT-enabled classrooms equipped with interactive smart boards, high-definition projectors, audio-visual systems, and internet connectivity for an engaging, technology-driven learning experience.

🌿

Medicinal Garden

Over 50 species of herbal and medicinal plants, providing a living laboratory for Pharmacognosy identification and research.

Student Welfare

Supporting Facilities

Beyond classrooms and laboratories, we provide ancillary services to support student life, transport, and overall well-being on campus.

  • Sports Complex: Facilities for indoor games (carrom, chess, table tennis) and outdoor grounds for cricket and volleyball.
  • Safety & Security: 24x7 CCTV monitoring, gate entry registries, and dedicated campus wardens.
